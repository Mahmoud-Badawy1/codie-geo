# Practical examples

- `/codie-geo`  → load existing profile or interview first-time user.
- `/codie-geo setup` → configure a clothing store, SaaS platform, clinic or nonprofit.
- `/codie-geo research` → investigate one fresh topic without writing the article.
- `/codie-geo create` → write article + Instagram + LinkedIn + X + Reddit + Facebook posts with native copy and hashtags.
- `/codie-geo all` → research → approval → create + five posts → QA; respect approval settings.
- `/codie-geo social turn this article into posts: [paste text or link]` → five platform-specific drafts, no new article.
- `/codie-geo instagram topic: healthy plant care` → just a caption + hashtag suggestions + carousel concept.
- `/codie-geo linkedin use a developer audience and explain a technical lesson` → LinkedIn only.
- `/codie-geo x make one post plus a thread on the release notes` → X only.
- `/codie-geo reddit create an educational discussion draft, disclose affiliation` → community-ready draft, no hashtags inserted.
- `/codie-geo facebook turn the guide into a practical tip for local families` → Facebook only.
- `/codie-geo channels` → see active channels and whether default or explicit selection.
- `/codie-geo set-channels instagram linkedin facebook` → remember three channels for that project (requires persistent profile).
- `/codie-geo hooks 10 alternatives for an honest article opening about water savings` → hooks only.
- `/codie-geo visuals design keyword searches for a fashion campaign` → twelve-layer visual-reference search + visual copy ideas.
- `/codie-geo copy draft a long and short campaign story from these verified customer interviews` → optional full storyselling copies.
- `/codie-geo qa check today's article and posts for style, source coverage and files` → QA.

No external connections, scheduled runs or publishing actions are magically installed by this skill.

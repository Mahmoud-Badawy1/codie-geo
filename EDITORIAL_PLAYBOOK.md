# Codie GEO editorial playbook

This package retains the previous Codie GEO writing specifications:

- `editorial/copywriting.md`: authentic reader-centered stories and copy, ethical hooks, useful outcomes, fewer predictable AI-style openings
- `editorial/source-prompt-adaptation.md`: adaptations of the user-provided writing and design brief; any sample book/business details are illustrative, never assumed true
- `editorial/visual-search.md`: 12-layer professional visual-reference keyword strategy
- `platforms/instagram.md`, `linkedin.md`, `x.md`, `reddit.md`, `facebook.md`: individual platform voice and format guidance

Avoid Arabic tatweel (U+0640), pointless horizontal dash separators, unsupported personal stories, fake endorsements, and text designed to game AI detectors. Never sacrifice evidence for copywriting effect. Use sources from the research brief, with verified dates and factual boundaries.

`/codie-geo create` produces an article plus the five social drafts unless the user requests a narrower scope. `/codie-geo research` stops at the research brief and does not silently write an article.

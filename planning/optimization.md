# Audit → research → improvement plan

This stage must follow the current website audit (or a user-supplied audit labeled with date and validity) and must connect every proposed action to evidence. Reuse findings from `audit/orchestration.md`, then perform targeted current research using official search engine documentation, original product specs/standards, reputable security/developer sources and carefully attributed community signals.

## Mandatory sequence

1. Baseline: extract verified observed findings, affected pages, confidence and business priorities; tag unknown / untested separately.
2. Benchmark: choose 3-5 real competitors or search-result peers and record their competing URL, content type, differentiator and evidence; don't presume competing content ranks without an actual observed SERP.
3. Content gap: map audience questions to weak/missing internal pages; cluster by intent and journey, find missing answer blocks, proof, examples, structured data, linking or multimedia.
4. Technical/authority remedies: formulate quick fixes for high-impact blocking issues, content answers and information architecture, metadata and structure, schema where eligible, evidence/E-E-A-T, crawler access only where appropriate; avoid SEO myths.
5. Produce a prioritized backlog: action, finding ID, source/evidence URL, expected benefit (qualitative unless measured), effort, dependencies, owner role, acceptance test, status and timeframe.
6. Sequence 0-14, 15-30, 31-60 and 61-90 day milestones; identify blockers and owner approvals. Separate experiments from recommended fixes.
7. Define verification: baseline date, concrete measures from GSC/GA4 if connected, direct SERP sample if available, content engagement or conversion measures, and a comparison window. No guaranteed ranks or conversions.
8. Plan topical research that serves gaps and fresh developments; use `workflows/research.md` for one or multiple briefs, check prior topics, and record credible citations.
9. Request approval only if the saved workflow requires it; never silently skip explicit approvals. Then send approved briefs to `workflows/content.md`, `workflows/social.md`, and `workflows/qa.md`.

## Deliverables

- `optimization-plan.md` with 90-day roadmap and dependency map.
- `prioritized-backlog.csv` with explicit observed/unknown differentiation.
- `content-gap-matrix.csv` linking search intent, target URL, competition, evidence and planned brief.
- `topic-calendar.csv` avoiding duplicate subjects and matching the project cadence.
- Report executive summary: baseline score with coverage/confidence, 3-5 key risks, fast wins, longer-term priorities, what will be measured, and a next audit date chosen or confirmed by user.

Research is not content creation. Do not draft a full article unless the user invoked `create` or `all` and approvals allow it.

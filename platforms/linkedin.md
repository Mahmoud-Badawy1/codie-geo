# LinkedIn: precise insight and earned credibility

Deliver: one ready-to-post professional narrative or mini-case with specific opening, real trade-off/lesson, sourced claim(s), practical takeaway, proportionate discussion question or CTA, 2-4 tightly relevant optional hashtags, optional evidence-driven carousel/document idea with slide titles, link/source placement, alt text, and 2 alternate openings.

Use smart professional language without business clichés, smug 'hot takes', fake founder memories or synthetic 'I learned the hard way' anecdotes. Cite/attribute product releases, industry data and case studies. Article-to-post adaptation should add a perspective, not republish the table of contents.

# Facebook: accessible practical conversation

Deliver: one concise but useful post with a relatable scene/problem and practical answer, 1-3 optional topical hashtags (zero allowed if they add nothing), clear link placement / CTA suitable to an authorized Page or Group, optional text-on-visual concept, alt text, 2 alternative hooks, and suggested conversation follow-up.

Adjust register to community and region. Avoid heavy sales pitch in Groups, fabricated 'our customer' stories, exaggerated alarmism or spammy share-tag-comment prompts. Provide enough context that it still helps someone who never opens the article.
